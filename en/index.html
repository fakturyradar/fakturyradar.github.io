KSeF invoices in Shortcuts

FakturyRadar adds KSeF actions to Shortcuts on iPhone and iPad. New invoices go where you need them, without opening KSeF.

  • No serversThe app connects to KSeF directly from your device. We never see your token or any invoice.
  • Runs on your phoneYour own automations in Shortcuts do the checking. FakturyRadar only provides the actions they use.
  • Your token stays on the deviceThe KSeF token is encrypted in this device's keychain. It never goes to iCloud or to other devices.
  • Nobody tracks youNo sign-up, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. The app collects no data.

What you can automate

  • A morning notification about new purchase invoices.
  • New invoices listed when you arrive at work.
  • PDFs of new invoices in your email inbox.
  • A daily PDF archive in iCloud Drive.
  • A monthly summary with gross totals.

Add any of five ready-made shortcuts with one tap in the app. Or build your own: the actions return PDF, CSV and JSON files, or text in your own template.

How it works

  1. Generate a token in the KSeF Taxpayer App and add the account in FakturyRadar.
  2. Add a ready-made shortcut or build your own from the FakturyRadar actions.
  3. Set up an automation in Shortcuts, for example every day at 8:00. From then on everything runs on its own, in the background.

Each invoice reaches an automation exactly once, even if a run is skipped. The PDF is the official KSeF visualization, generated on the device.

FakturyRadar is an independent app, not affiliated with the Polish Ministry of Finance. It connects to KSeF through the public API.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 27. Read the guide or get in touch.
