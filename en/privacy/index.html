Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: FakturyRadar has no servers and collects no data. The app connects only to KSeF, directly from your device. Your token, accounts, invoices and activity history stay on the device where you added them. There are no user accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

Who is responsible

FakturyRadar is made by Volodymyr Prokopchuk. Contact: fakturyradar@gmail.com.

Data on your device

The app keeps the data below only on the device where the account was added. It is never sent to us or synced to your other devices. A device backup made by Apple may include the accounts and history, but never the token: after restoring to a new device you paste it again.

DataWhere and how long
KSeF token and session tokensThe device's keychain, encrypted, this device only. Removed with the account.
Accounts (name, tax ID, environment)App storage, until you remove the account.
Activity history with invoice summaries (number, seller, amount, due date)App storage, 90 days. You can clear it in Settings.
Downloaded invoice files (XML)The app's cache, protected by the system, 90 days.
"Since last run" progress and KSeF request limitsApp storage, until you clear the cache in Settings.

Network connections

The app connects only to the Ministry of Finance's KSeF servers (api.ksef.mf.gov.pl, plus the TEST and DEMO environments for test accounts) to sign in with your token and download your invoices. The Ministry of Finance is responsible for data processed in KSeF, under its own terms.

There is no FakturyRadar server relaying or observing these connections.

Your shortcuts and iCloud Drive

You decide where your shortcuts send the results of the actions, such as email, messages or files. Those apps and services process that data, not FakturyRadar.

The app creates a FakturyRadar folder in your iCloud Drive for a PDF archive. Files your shortcuts save there are stored by Apple in your iCloud account. We have no access to them.

Apple and the App Store

Apple handles purchases and downloads under Apple's privacy policy. We receive only aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics from Apple. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to us.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, ads, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking libraries, and never links your data with data from other apps or websites. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply. Email is handled by Gmail (Google). We delete correspondence once it is no longer needed. Never send us your KSeF token or invoice contents.

Deleting your data

  • Removing an account in the app deletes its token from the keychain and the account's data.
  • "Clear activity" and "Clear cache" in Settings remove the history and search progress.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Remove your accounts in the app first.
  • You can also revoke the token at any time in the KSeF Taxpayer App.

Your rights

For email correspondence, you have the rights given by the GDPR: access, correction, deletion and restriction of processing, and a complaint to the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych). No other data reaches us, so there is nothing for us to provide or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Any feature that would send data anywhere other than KSeF will be described here before it ships.
